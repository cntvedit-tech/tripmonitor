🎯 What Kerala Offers

Unforgettable Kerala Experiences

Kerala has something magical for every kind of traveller.

🚤

Houseboat Cruises

Glide through emerald backwaters on traditional kettuvallam houseboats with gourmet Kerala meals.

🐘

Wildlife Safaris

Spot elephants, tigers and rare birds in Periyar, Wayanad and Silent Valley national parks.

🧖

Ayurvedic Wellness

Rejuvenate with authentic Kerala Ayurveda treatments and yoga retreats by serene backwaters.

🎭

Cultural Festivals

Witness Thrissur Pooram, Kathakali dance, Onam celebrations and the Snake Boat Race.

🏄

Beach Activities

Surf, kayak and snorkel at Kovalam, Varkala, Marari and Bekal beaches along the Arabian Sea.

🍛

Kerala Cuisine

Savour traditional Sadhya, fresh seafood curries, appam with stew and toddy with spicy snacks.

⛵

Fishing Villages

Meet local fishermen, watch Chinese fishing nets at dawn and enjoy fresh catch meals at Fort Kochi.

🌄

Trekking & Camping

Trek through Meesapulimala, Chembra Peak and Agasthyarkoodam in the Western Ghats.
